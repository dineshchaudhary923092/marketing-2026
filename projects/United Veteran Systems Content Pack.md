## Visual Read
- Warm housing imagery and American visual cues immediately frame the category without making the design feel loud or theatrical.
- The hero section positions the opportunity clearly for property owners and investors instead of leading with vague branding.
- Mid-page sections show strong strategic storytelling: problem, solution, approach, support structure, and long-term impact.
- The later screens strengthen conversion by adding qualification forms, metrics, FAQs, and a focused CTA section.
- The overall design feels more like a complete website system than a single landing page, with trust built through structure, pacing, and message clarity.
