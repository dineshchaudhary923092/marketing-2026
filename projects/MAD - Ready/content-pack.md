## LinkedIn
**Post**

We designed MAD as a dance studio website and management system built around movement, community, and booking clarity.

The public-facing side introduces the brand through classes, workshops, events, and membership options, while the dashboard side supports subscriptions, bookings, payments, user growth, and studio operations. The goal was to give the experience enough energy to feel like dance, while keeping the product side structured enough to support everyday use.

This kind of project works best when brand expression and product logic move together. The site has to inspire people to join, and the platform has to make signups, scheduling, and management feel easy.
