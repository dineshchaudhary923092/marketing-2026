**Post**

Farmland is a highly specialized asset class. High-performing investment platforms must make it readable.

We designed Farmstax to bridge the gap between complex agricultural investment models and everyday investors. The platform features a public-facing website and a secure private dashboard that simplifies workflows for buyers, sellers, and administrators across leasing and partial purchases.

The visual direction uses a clean, light grey layout with soft teal accents and a comparative table comparing farmland yields against stocks, bonds, and gold. This approach establishes credibility without relying on aggressive marketing claims.
