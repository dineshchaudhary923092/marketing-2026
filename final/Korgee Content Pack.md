## LinkedIn
**Post**

We designed Korgee as a Korean learning product for people who want language they can actually use in real situations.

Instead of leaning on abstract grammar-first lessons, the experience focuses on everyday travel moments like airport arrivals, subway rides, ordering food, and asking for help. The product needed to feel approachable, practical, and easy to follow for users preparing for time in Korea.

The goal was simple: make language learning feel more useful from the start.
