# Second Aid App Social Media Content Pack

## Project Positioning
- **Project:** Second Aid App
- **Angle:** Healthtech mobile app UI for patients and doctors with a stronger focus on consultation journeys, doctor-side workflows, patient records, onboarding, profile flows, and supporting healthcare actions
- **Voice:** Clear, calm, structured, trust-led
- **Perspective:** We
- **Language:** English only
- **Emojis:** None

## Core Narrative
- **Primary story:** A healthcare mobile product experience designed to make consultations, records, patient management, and day-to-day doctor and patient actions easier to understand and easier to use.
- **Business relevance:** The work supports healthcare product trust through quieter interfaces, readable typography, clearer screen states, and flows that reduce friction instead of adding noise.
- **Social-first logic:** The asset set is broad, so social content should be split by story rather than forcing every screen into one generic caption. The strongest themes in this version are consultation flow, doctor workflow, patient records, profile handling, and healthcare access.

## Visual Read
- Clean monochrome mobile UI with soft grey-blue surfaces and minimal color accents
- Strong editorial typography that gives the interface a distinct product character
- Mockups use stone, concrete, and minimal studio backgrounds for a more premium presentation
- Screens cover both broader product utility and tighter feature-level healthcare flows

## Content Angles by Asset Group

### H Group — Broader Product Story
These assets work best for the wider product narrative: healthcare access, app structure, trusted doctor discovery, appointment handling, records, and the system-level patient-doctor experience.

### C Group — Consultation and Clinical Workflow
These assets work best for consultation journeys, test handling, doctor-side patient management, consultation lists, and operational healthcare actions.

### CH Group — Consultation Detail and Real Interaction
These assets are stronger for consultation detail views, patient information, doctor advice, chat interaction, consultation fee flow, and live healthcare coordination.
